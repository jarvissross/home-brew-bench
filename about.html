About Home Brew Bench

Home Brew Bench publishes buying guides for home coffee equipment: grinders, kettles, scales, brewers and gifts. Our goal is to help you choose the right tool for how you actually brew, without the hype.

How we research

  • We do not test products in person. Our guides are research-based. We compare manufacturer specifications, retailer listings and published reviews from outlets that did test the products.
  • Every guide lists its sources at the bottom of the page, so you can check our work.
  • We flag disagreements. When sources disagree on a spec, we say so rather than picking one quietly.
  • Prices are dated snapshots. Each price we show comes from a named retailer or publication on a stated date, or is marked "check current price". Prices change often, so check the retailer before you buy.
  • We recommend by use case. Picks are based on fit for a particular brew method and budget.
  • We don't publish reviews or testimonials we can't verify.

AI assistance

Our guides are researched and drafted with the help of AI tools, then checked against the cited sources before publishing. We disclose this at the top of every guide.
