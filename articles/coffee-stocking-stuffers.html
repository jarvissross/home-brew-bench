Before you buy: two quick checks

What do they already brew with? A gift that fits their routine gets used. Cleaning tablets and a brush are only useful to someone with a burr grinder. A frother suits milk drinkers. A dripper or a cold brew pot adds a new method, which works best for someone who likes to tinker.

Ground or whole bean? The V60, the French press and the cold brew pot all work with pre-ground coffee, but each wants a different grind: medium-fine for the V60, coarse for the French press and cold brew. If they don't own a grinder, pair the gift with a bag of coffee ground for that method, or see our burr vs blade grinder explainer if a grinder is on your list instead.

Brewers under $25

Hario V60-01 plastic dripper

The V60 is a cone dripper with one large hole and spiral ribs. The 01 is the small size: Eight Ounce Coffee lists it for 1–2 cups, with an acrylonitrile-styrene resin body and a polypropylene measuring spoon in the box, at $12.99 on October 20, 2026 (its page showed both an add-to-cart button and a back-in-stock alert, so stock was unclear). It sits on a mug, so there's nothing else to buy except filters. Our pour-over coffee maker guide compares the V60 with the Kalita Wave, Chemex and others if you're choosing between them; that guide covers the larger 02 size, which suits two cups.

Good to know: the V60 is a fast-draining cone, so pour speed and grind size change the cup more than with a flat-bottom dripper. For a beginner, that's part of the fun or part of the frustration. A gooseneck kettle helps, but isn't required to start.

Hario Mizudashi Cold Brew Coffee Pot

This is the cheapest brewer in this guide and the easiest to use. You put coarse grounds in the fine mesh basket, fill the glass pot with water and leave it in the fridge. Prima Coffee listed it at $11.75 on sale (MSRP $19.50) on October 20, 2026, in 600 ml and 1000 ml sizes; check which size the sale price applies to. Prima's own recipe steeps it for about 18 hours. Bodum Brazil French press (34 oz)

The Brazil uses the same basic design as Bodum's pricier Chambord: a borosilicate glass carafe and a steel mesh plunger, with a BPA-free plastic frame instead of a metal one. A Slickdeals post from July 2026 showed the 34 oz size at $19.99 at Target, and Ace Hardware listed it at $24.99 in a search snapshot. A glass carafe can break, and mesh lets some fine sediment through. Our French press guide covers insulated and lower-sediment alternatives.

FAQ

What coffee gifts actually fit in a stocking?

Filters, cleaning tablets, the brush, the V60-01 and a handheld frother are all small. The book fits a large stocking. The cold brew pot and the French press are better under the tree.

Do V60-02 filters fit a V60-01?

Buy filters that match the dripper size. Hario filters are sold by size (01, 02 and 03), and retailers such as Prima Coffee name the dripper size each pack is for.

Are grinder cleaning tablets safe?

Whole Latte Love's Grindz listing says the tablets use edible, food-safe ingredients, but also says they're for stand-alone grinders only and not super-automatic machines. Check the grinder maker's manual, since some manufacturers give their own cleaning instructions.

When should I order holiday stocking stuffers?

Several items here showed unclear or sold-out stock when we checked in October, so earlier is safer. Small specialty retailers can sell out of a single size or color before the big stores do.
