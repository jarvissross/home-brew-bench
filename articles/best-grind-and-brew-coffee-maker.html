A grind-and-brew coffee maker grinds whole beans right before it brews, so you get fresher coffee without a separate grinder on the counter. The catch is that the grinder is the part that varies most. Some of these machines use burr grinders with several settings; the cheapest ones use a blade that chops beans unevenly. This guide compares eight machines on the things that matter for daily use: grinder type, carafe, single-cup brewing, cleaning and price.

How to choose a grind-and-brew coffee maker

1. Burr or blade grinder

This is the biggest quality difference between these machines. A burr grinder crushes beans between two burrs set a fixed distance apart, so the grounds come out a more even size. A blade grinder spins a blade and chops the beans, so you get a mix of fine dust and larger pieces, and the longer it runs the finer it gets. Uneven grounds extract unevenly, which can taste bitter and sour at the same time. Six of the eight machines here use burrs. The Cuisinart DGB-400 uses a blade, and BLACK+DECKER doesn't say which type the CM5000B uses. Our burr vs blade grinder explainer covers the difference in more detail.

2. Thermal or glass carafe

A glass carafe sits on a hot plate that keeps cooking the coffee, which is fine if the pot goes in half an hour. A thermal carafe has no hot plate and holds heat on its own. If you drink a pot slowly through the morning, pick thermal: the Café, Breville, GE Profile and Cuisinart DGB-900BC all have one. The same trade-off is covered in our drip coffee maker guide.

3. One cup or a full pot

If you often brew one mug, look for a real single-serve mode rather than a small-batch setting on a big carafe. The Café and GE Profile brew 6 to 24 oz into a mug or travel mug, and the Breville brews into tall cups and short travel mugs. The Cuisinart DGB-800 and DGB-900BC and the KRUPS let you tell the grinder how many cups to grind for, which avoids grinding a full pot's worth of beans for a small batch.

4. Cleaning

In a grind-and-brew machine the grinder sits close to the hot water, and grounds travel from the grinder to the filter basket. Buyer comments we read mention steam reaching the hopper and grounds clumping (Cuisinart DGB-400), grinder noise (Cuisinart DGB-900BC), and leaks or brewing faults on some units (Breville). Plan on brushing out the grinder area regularly. The Café includes a cleaning brush and a replaceable burr; the Breville includes a cleaning brush and a gold-tone filter.

5. Hopper size and whether you can skip the grinder

Hoppers here hold roughly 6.7 oz to half a pound of beans. A bigger hopper means fewer refills, but beans sitting in a hopper for days go stale faster than beans in an airtight container (see our coffee canister guide). Several of these machines also let you turn the grinder off and use pre-ground coffee: the GE Profile, BLACK+DECKER and Breville listings say so directly.

Grind-and-brew vs a separate grinder

An all-in-one saves counter space and a step in the morning, and with a timer you can wake up to coffee ground minutes earlier. A separate grinder and brewer cost more space but give you more grind settings, let you replace one part without the other, and keep brewing steam away from the grinder. If you're spending Breville or Café money anyway, compare that with pairing a burr grinder from our under-$200 grinder guide and a brewer from our drip guide at current prices. If you want one machine and one button, a burr grind-and-brew is the better choice.

FAQ

Are grind and brew coffee makers worth it?

They're worth it if you want freshly ground coffee with one machine and a timer. Choose a burr model if taste matters to you. If you already own a good grinder, a plain drip machine is simpler and has fewer parts to clean.

Can you use pre-ground coffee in a grind and brew machine?

Many can. The GE Profile, Breville and BLACK+DECKER listings mention a grinder-off or pre-ground option. Check the manual for your model.

Is any grind and brew coffee maker SCA certified?

When we read the SCA certified home brewer list on October 20, 2026, it included grind-and-brew models from Café (Specialty Grind and Brew) and GE Profile (Smart Grind and Brew). The list changes, so check sca.coffee for the current version.

How do I keep the grinder from clogging?

Brush out the grinder outlet regularly, keep the hopper lid closed, and follow the cleaning steps in your manual. Some buyers of the Cuisinart DGB-400 reported steam reaching the hopper and grounds clumping.

How long do beans stay fresh in the hopper?

A hopper isn't a storage container. Fill it with what you'll use in a few days and keep the rest sealed, away from light and heat. Our coffee canister guide covers storage.
