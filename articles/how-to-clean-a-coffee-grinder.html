Coffee oils and fine grounds build up in the burrs and chute of any grinder. The sources we read describe two routines: a quick dry clean, and a tablet run every couple of months. This guide covers both and which tools suit which grinder. How to clean with tablets

Clive Coffee's support page gives this routine for Grindz and says to do it "every couple of months":

  1. Empty the hopper of any old coffee and set the grinder 3 to 4 steps coarser than usual.
  2. Pour one 35 g packet of Grindz into the empty grinder.
  3. Grind the whole packet and discard the output.
  4. Add about 40 g (4 to 5 tablespoons) of old coffee and grind it to flush leftover cleaner from the burrs. Discard it.

Tools & Toys describes the bottle version slightly differently: one capful into the grinder at a medium setting, then keep running coffee through until no cleaner dust comes out, then wipe the surfaces. Cleaner dust is normal. The two instructions differ on amounts and setting, so follow the directions printed on your product.

How to clean by hand (brush and air)

The manual routine in our results: remove the hopper and empty the beans, wipe the hopper with a clean, dry towel, then clear the burrs and grind chute with a brush and compressed air or a blower (or a vacuum with a nozzle), and sweep out the grounds bin. Avoid damp towels, because moisture can make grounds stick to the burrs and cause jams. A deeper clean can mean disassembling the grinder; use the manual's own instructions for that. We found no authoritative source on how often to do the dry clean, so use your manual's schedule.

FAQ

How often should I clean my grinder?

Clive Coffee suggests running Grindz every couple of months. We found no solid source for brush-cleaning frequency; follow your manual.

Do I need to take the grinder apart?

Not for the tablet method, per Clive Coffee, Tools & Toys and Urnex. A deeper clean may need disassembly per your manual.

Is it safe for any grinder?

No. Tools & Toys says Grindz is for standalone machines and could damage a super-automatic with a built-in grinder. Check your grinder's manual.

Will cleaning change the taste?

The Tools & Toys reviewer says it noticeably improved their coffee's taste. That is one person's report, not something we tested.
