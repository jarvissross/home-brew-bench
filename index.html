How we work: We don't test products in person. Our guides compare manufacturer specs, retailer listings and published reviews from outlets that did test them, and every guide lists its sources. Guides are researched and drafted with AI assistance. Read more on our about and disclosure page.

Coffee gear is full of jargon and marketing claims. Our guides do the reading for you: we collect the published specs, note where sources disagree, explain the tradeoffs in plain English, and recommend a pick for each kind of brewer. Prices are dated snapshots from named retailers, so you can see exactly where they came from.

Where to start

If you're upgrading from pre-ground coffee, a common upgrade order is: a burr grinder first, then a scale, then a gooseneck kettle if you brew pour-over (or a regular electric kettle for tea and French press). Want the simplest brewer? See our French press guide. Thinking about espresso? Start with our beginner espresso machine guide, and budget for a grinder too: see espresso grinders under $300. Want lattes without an espresso machine? See our milk frother guide, and for strong coffee on the stove, our moka pot guide. To keep beans fresh between brews, see our coffee canister guide. Brewing away from home? See our travel coffee maker guide, and for carrying it, our insulated travel mug guide. Prefer to keep a cup warm at your desk? See our heated mug guide. Just want a good pot with less fuss? See our drip coffee maker guide, or for fresh-ground coffee from one machine, our grind and brew guide.
