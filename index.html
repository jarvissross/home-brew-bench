How we work: We don't test products in person. Our guides compare manufacturer specs, retailer listings and published reviews from outlets that did test them, and every guide lists its sources. Guides are researched and drafted with AI assistance. Read more on our about and disclosure page.

Coffee gear is full of jargon and marketing claims. Our guides do the reading for you: we collect the published specs, note where sources disagree, explain the tradeoffs in plain English, and recommend a pick for each kind of brewer. Prices are dated snapshots from named retailers, so you can see exactly where they came from.
